# Material atlas asset credits and licenses

`materials/atlas.jpg` and `materials/atlas-normal.jpg` were assembled for this project by `tools/build-materials.py`. The generator downloads the following source archives temporarily, samples their color maps, and does not retain the archives or other PBR maps in the repository:

|---|---|---|---|
| Carpet 012 | [ambientCG Carpet 012](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Carpet012) | [Carpet012_1K-JPG.zip](https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Carpet012_1K-JPG.zip), `_Color.jpg` | [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| Wood 051 | [ambientCG Wood 051](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood051) | [Wood051_1K-JPG.zip](https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Wood051_1K-JPG.zip), `_Color.jpg` | [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |

ambientCG's [license page](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) states that its downloadable assets are provided under CC0 1.0 and may be copied, modified, distributed, and used commercially without permission or attribution. The Carpet 012 and Wood 051 asset pages also state that all ambientCG assets are released under CC0.

The wallpaper's damask ornament, burgundy-and-gold carpet repeat, and aged plaster appearance are original procedural artwork authored for this project in the generator. The wood retains and color-adjusts the ambientCG Wood 051 color-map grain. The carpet's pile detail is sampled from ambientCG Carpet 012 and recolored beneath an original motif. The normal atlas is derived from the generated color atlas by the same script. No logos or extra PBR maps are included.
